## Why do I feel so tired now that the pressure is off?

For years your system ran on urgency. Late nights, constant alerts, decisions on the move. That kind of pace can carry you a long way. When it stops suddenly, the tiredness you postponed can arrive all at once.

Founders often describe it as hitting a wall a few weeks after closing, when the paperwork is finished and the messages dry up.

## Is it normal to get ill or low after a big deal?

Many founders say so. A change this large touches sleep, appetite and mood. We are not doctors and cannot tell you what is behind your own symptoms. If something feels wrong, or it does not ease, please talk to a medical professional. That is always the right first step.

## What can I do to look after my energy?

Keep it simple. Here are a few things founders tell us have helped:

- Regular sleep and wake times, even without an alarm to obey.
- Daylight and a walk, most days.
- Fewer commitments at first, so you are not rebuilding the pressure.
- One thing a week that has nothing to do with work.

### Don't turn recovery into a project

It is easy to replace the company with a strict wellness plan. Try to treat this period as space, not another goal to hit.
