## Why do I feel pressure to start something new right away?

An empty calendar can feel uncomfortable after years of being in demand. People ask what is next, and "nothing yet" feels like a weak answer. So many founders say yes quickly just to feel useful again.

## What is the risk of filling my calendar too fast?

You can rebuild the very life you just left. A new company, a heavy board schedule, a stack of advisory roles. All of it is fine in itself. But if you take it on to avoid a quiet stretch, you may find yourself just as stretched as before, with less meaning attached.

### Say yes slowly

A useful rule of thumb: if you cannot say why you want it in one honest sentence, wait a week.

## How do I choose on purpose?

Start with the feeling, not the job title. Ask:

- How do I want my mornings to feel?
- Who do I want to spend most of my time with?
- What would I do if nobody was watching?
- What am I saying yes to because I am afraid to say no?

Then try small experiments. A month on a project, a few conversations, a short trip. See what gives you energy and what drains it.

This is about direction and pace. It is not financial advice, and decisions about money, tax or investments should always involve a qualified professional.
