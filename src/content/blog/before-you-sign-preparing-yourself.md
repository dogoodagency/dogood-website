## Why prepare myself if the company is what is being sold?

Because you are leaving too. For years the company set your routine, your status and a lot of your sense of purpose. When it goes, all of that goes with it on the same day. The deal covers the company. Nothing in the paperwork covers you.

## When is the right time to start?

Earlier than feels necessary. Many founders tell us they would have liked to start a year before the sale. Once a deal is moving, every hour is taken by the process, and there is little room left to think about anything else.

If you are only thinking about selling, that is a good moment. The personal side is easiest to shape before the deal sets the timeline. Our [Exit-Prep page](/exit-prep) explains how we work with founders at this stage.

## What does preparing yourself actually involve?

It is simpler than it sounds. A few questions, asked honestly:

- What do I want a normal Tuesday to look like six months after closing?
- Who am I when nobody calls me the CEO?
- Who at home needs to be part of this conversation?
- What am I afraid will be missing?
