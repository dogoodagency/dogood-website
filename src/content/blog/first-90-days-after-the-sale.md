## Why do I feel flat after something I worked towards for years?

Because the goal that held you up has gone. For a long time the exit was the destination. Once you arrive, there is a gap where the drive used to be. This is not ingratitude. Founders in our community describe it as a strange mix of relief and emptiness.

## What happens to my days once the company is gone?

The structure goes first. No stand-ups, no urgent messages, no one waiting on a decision. Some founders enjoy this for a week or two. Then the quiet starts to feel loud.

You may also notice how many of your habits were built around the company. Even small things, like how you start the morning, can feel unanchored.

### The "should" voices

Friends say you must be thrilled. Your own head says you should already know what is next. Neither is a good guide in these early weeks.

## What helps in the first three months?

Small, steady things tend to help more than big moves:

- A simple weekly rhythm, even if it is only three fixed points.
- Time outside, and time with people who do not ask about the deal.
- Saying out loud how it actually feels, to one or two people you trust.
- Pausing on big commitments until you can see more clearly.

None of this is advice about money or health. It is just what founders tell us has helped.
