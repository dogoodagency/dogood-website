# 1:1 Clarity Sprints (3 months)

For founders + select founder-like executives

Thank you – You're booked

What we’ll do in your sprints:

Each sprint is 60 minutes and stays fluid based on what’s most relevant in the moment. The flow follows a consistent arc:

### 1) Clarity: We start by analysing the issue and making sure you see where you’re holding a story as if it’s reality. The goal is to restore the core lever: you’re in control of how you emotionally respond to a situation — and you can choose differently.

###

### 2) Decision: Once the issue is clear, we build the map moving forward. Then we re-route the issue from “problem” to opportunity: What is the distinction you need? The output here is a clean decision and the next step.

###

### 3) Execution: We move from insight into your system:

- Integration through reflections to ground insights into the physical/neurological nervous system
- Habit rerouting: we work with thought patterns / self-talk → emotional responses → actions
- We look at patterns on a 7‑day week basis and practice the new responses — to create psychological safety in the new decisions and habits.

Most clients feel lighter, regain clarity, and can take new empowered actions from that frame.

## How to prepare for each sprint: (Copy/paste and answer these in a note)

1. What’s the one thing that’s most “alive” right now?
2. What decision are you avoiding or second‑guessing?
3. What keeps breaking down after you “know what to do”?
4. What’s the typical chain of behaviours (from self‑talk → emotion → action)?

Bring that note into the session.
