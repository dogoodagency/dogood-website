What Happens Next

You'll receive two emails shortly.

Email 1: The ClarityValue Shift™

Our foundational framework for understanding where you are and what's driving the crisis. Review this while we assess your application — it will give you clarity on the process ahead.

Email 2: Application Confirmation

A copy of your responses for your records, which can help you prepare for the FRP-Fit Call, if we're a fit.

Next Steps

If your application shows strong alignment with the FRP, we'll schedule the FRP-Fit Call. This isn't a sales call. It's a mutual assessment to determine if the Founder Reclamation Partnership is the right path for where you are right now.

Not every founder who applies will be accepted.

We work with a limited number of clients at a time, and only those who meet specific readiness and fit criteria. If it isn't a fit, it's not a reflection on you – it simply means the timing, crisis stage, or partnership structure doesn't align with what you need right now (honestly, it's probably a good thing).

In the Meantime

Check your inbox for the ClarityValue Shift™. Read it. Sit with it. It's designed to help you see what's actually happening beneath the surface — regardless of whether we work together.
