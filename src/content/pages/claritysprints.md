# 1:1 Clarity Sprints (3 months)

Founders rarely need more information. They need clarity. Fast. 1:1 ClaritySprints help you make decisions and move forward in business

and life (they’re the same system).

Each session is a focused working block. We’ll:

- Find the real constraint (not the surface problem)
- Get to the decision you know needs to happen
- Leave with clear action points for the next 7 days

What we work on:

- Business: offer clarity, positioning, acquisition/lead gen, strategy
- Leadership: boundaries, team dynamics, decision-making
- Life: stress/emotion load, relationships, identity, health & money concerns

This is for you if…

- You want focused 1:1 support (not a structured FRP program)
- You need clarity + execution (not just insights)
- You have decision fog and feel reactive instead of clear
- You’re high-performing externally, but internally you’re paying for it (health/relationship/identity strain)

This is not for…

- FRP-fit founders (FRP routing)
- Team / culture / ops delivery work (separate service)
- Therapy, crisis care, or medical support

For new clients, here’s how booking works:

First, we’ll book 12 sessions. These sessions will happen 4 times each month. You start by booking the very first session today. We’ll then schedule the other 11 sessions for the same day of the week + time. After these 12 sessions, we'll decide if we want to continue working together. Most clients stay with us for 12 months and longer.

Important: (FRP routing)

If you’re a founder (post-exit) in crisis / identity collapse (health scare, panic spiral, divorce in motion, can’t-function volatility), do not book on this page.
