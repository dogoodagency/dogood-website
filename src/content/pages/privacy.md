At DoGood Agency, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website.

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### Automatically Collected Information

Our website host processes basic technical data (IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, referring page) to deliver the site and keep it secure.

With your consent, we use analytics and heat-map tools to understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, clicks, scrolling, device and approximate location), an advertising measurement tool to see, in aggregate, what kinds of professionals visit and to measure advertising, and a visitor insights tool that shows which companies visit our site and which pages they read. The heat-map tool records anonymized sessions and masks text you type. Analytics and heat-map tools only run after you accept analytics cookies; advertising measurement and visitor insights only run after you accept advertisement cookies in our cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time.

### Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily submit through:

- Contact forms or email inquiries
- Program applications
- Newsletter subscriptions
- Live chat messages
- Call bookings
- Forms you fill in
- The newsletter sign-up
- Client onboarding processes

This may include your name, email address, company name, job title, phone number, and any other information you choose to share.

### Payment Information

If you purchase services from us, we collect payment information through secure third-party processors (bank transfer through our bank-transfer provider, or card payment through our card-payment provider). We do not store your full credit card details on our servers.

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use collected information to:

- Operate and improve our website
- Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
- Send updates about our services (with your consent)
- Comply with legal obligations
- Prevent fraud and maintain security

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. INFORMATION SHARING

### When We Share Information

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

We share data only with service providers who help us run DoGood, under data processing terms: website hosting, cookie consent, live chat, call booking, online forms, email newsletter, customer records, e-signature, email and office tools, our podcast player, analytics and heat-map tools, an advertising measurement tool and a visitor insights tool. Payments are handled by our bank-transfer provider and our card-payment provider. A current list of providers is available on request at hiya@dogoodagency.co.

Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government regulation, or if necessary to protect our rights, prevent fraud, or ensure safety.

Business Transfers: If DoGood Agency is acquired or merged with another company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Aggregate Data: We may share anonymized, aggregate statistics about our website visitors with third parties for business purposes. This data cannot identify individual users.

### Legal basis

We handle data to answer you and to prepare or perform a contract, because the law requires it (for example accounting), with your consent (newsletter, and functional, analytics and advertisement cookies), or for our legitimate interest in running a secure site.

### Transfers

Some providers, including our payment providers, process data outside the EU/EEA; where they do, we rely on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 4. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

You have the right to:

- Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- Correction: Request that we update or correct inaccurate information
- Deletion: Request that we delete your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
- Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
- Cookie Control: Manage or withdraw cookie consent through our cookie banner
- Restriction, objection and data portability
- The right to complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon)

To exercise these rights, contact us at: [hiya@dogoodagency.co](mailto:hiya@dogoodagency.co)

## 5. COOKIES

COOKIES: We use cookies to run the site and live chat. The live-chat provider loads on every visit without waiting for functional-cookie consent. Analytics and heat-map tools run only after analytics consent, and advertising measurement and a visitor insights tool run only after advertisement consent. For details and to change your choices, see our [Cookies Policy](/cookies).

## 6. DATA SECURITY

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

- Secure SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
- Limited employee access to personal data
- Secure third-party service providers

However, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. DATA RETENTION
