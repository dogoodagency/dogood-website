# Internal Sync – 45 min

This calendar is for internal coordination & advisor syncs only. If you received this link directly from DoGood, you’re in the right place.

For DoGood staff, coaches, associates, and collaborators.

Use this to:

- Align on priorities, timelines, and deliverables
- Unblock anything stuck operationally
- Confirm next actions and deadlines so nothing drifts
- Clarify the direction of the project or other internal initiatives.

Prep (2 minutes)

- Bring questions, links/files we’ll reference
- Add a 1–2 line agenda in the calendar notes so we can move fast
