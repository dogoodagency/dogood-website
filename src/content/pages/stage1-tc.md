# Read before you Submit

To keep things simple and clean, I confirm that:

- This is an application for Stage 1: The Reset (30 days)
- If accepted, the next step is a 45‑minute conversation (no pitch—just fit + clarity)
- I can protect 5–6 hours/week for the next 30 days
- Stage 2 is invite-only and we’ll know by Week 3 whether we continue
- This is confidential, and spots are limited

Typical fit for FRP Stage 1 (The Intensive)

FRP is intentionally narrow. It’s designed for founders who match all three:

- Founder still running or post-exit / between companies with high liquidity
- Equity founder only: not for non-founder CEOs or senior leaders
- Founder crisis focus: identity, health, and relationship strain (not primarily business-performance issues)

If you don’t match this profile, you’ll likely get a better (faster, cleaner) outcome from a Clarity Sprint instead.
