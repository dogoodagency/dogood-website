## D. Warning signals to watch
If you start experiencing any of the below consistently, it usually means you’re drifting into the next higher Pressure Score Index (High Strain).​ This is when the nervous system starts needing significantly more effort to maintain the same output.

1

You delay more than one conversation/decision "because of how you feel." That means your behavior is now being regulated by pressure, not values.

2

Your private self-talk gets sharper and faster. "I should be able to handle this. What's wrong with me?" Internal pressure compounds before it shows up externally.

3

Rest stops restoring you. You rest, but don't feel refreshed. Early indicator your recovery inputs aren't keeping pace with outputs.

4

Relationships feel slightly more transactional. Not a rupture — just less warmth, less curiosity, less repair. You're efficient, but less present.

5

You "micro-dose" stimulation to stay level. More caffeine, more work, more scrolling, etc. Founders self-medicate the rising pressure without realizing it.

6

Decision fatigue shows up earlier in the day. Small decisions feel disproportionately heavy. That's often the first sign your mental bandwidth is under higher pressure load.
