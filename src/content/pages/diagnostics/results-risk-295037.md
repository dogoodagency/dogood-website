How we define our 4 metrics:

1. Cost signals (last 14 days)

You were asked to choose what the “cost” has looked like lately — how the pressure shows up in real life (body / nervous system, mood / baseline, relationships, identity, functioning).

2. Avoidance signals

You were asked whether you’ve been avoiding at least one meaningful conversation or decision because of how you felt. That matters because avoidance is often the first “quiet” marker of drift.

You were asked how you respond when the path forward requires discomfort. This isn’t a moral judgment. It’s a pressure signal: do you move, delay, negotiate it down, or need structure and accountability?

## D. Warning signals to watch
These are not typical PSI band 5 experiences. These are signs to watch out for to not move beyond acute depletion into territory where this diagnostic and FRP can no longer help you.

1

Severe physical exhaustion. Sleeping 12+ hours yet exhausted. Getting out of bed feels physically impossible. Persistent unexplained pain. Body feels like concrete.

2

Complete loss of drive and purpose. Nothing feels real or meaningful anymore. Feeding yourself feels pointless. Everything feels flat and empty.

3

Severe isolation. Haven't left your house in days or weeks. Seeing people feels unbearable. Stopped responding to messages. Alone, and you prefer it.

4

Basic functions stopped. Environment deteriorating (dishes, laundry, space chaotic). Can't track conversations. Lose hours. Days blur. Smallest tasks feel like mountains.

Important: If any of the above describe your reality right now, FRP and Expedited Start can't help you. We recommend real-time, local care from professionals who can offer immediate support.

If none of the above are true — but everything just feels impossibly heavy, you're barely functioning, and you can't remember the last time you felt like yourself — that's what Expedited Start is for. Next step: Apply for Stage 1 and complete the FRP‑Fit Call.

We can help with severe depletion. We can't help with crisis-level physical or psychological symptoms that require immediate medical care.
