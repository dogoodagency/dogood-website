## B. Nutshell Essentials
Based on what you indicated, you're showing sustained high-strain signals that typically correlate with function beginning to slip — not catastrophically, but measurably.

That means:

Decisions take longer or feel heavier than they should

Relationships are transactional or brittle (repair is slow, warmth is scheduled)

Rest doesn't restore you anymore (you sleep, but wake tired)

Avoidance isn't occasional — it's your default operating system

The dangerous part isn't that you're struggling. The dangerous part is that you're still performing well enough externally that it feels justifiable to keep pushing.

But internally, something critical shifted: your nervous system is now spending more energy compensating than it is restoring.

This is intervention territory — not crisis, but no longer prevention either.

The goal isn't to "power through." The goal is to contain the strain, map what's driving it, and create one corrective pathway before function degrades further and repair becomes significantly harder.

## D. Warning signals to watch
If you start experiencing any of the below consistently, it usually means you’re drifting into the next higher Pressure Score Index.​ This is when external function starts failing visibly, and others begin asking if you're okay.

1

Major decisions are delayed because deciding feels impossible. Your judgment works, but access to it is intermittent — a nervous system signal, not a competence issue.

2

You can't remember the last time you felt genuinely calm. Not "fine" or "managing" — actually calm. If you can't recall it easily, your baseline shifted without you noticing.

3

Someone close to you said something — and you overreacted. Not once. Repeatedly. The gap between stimulus and your response is narrowing dangerously.

4

You started isolating (even slightly). Skipping calls. Shorter replies. Less warmth. You're not avoiding people — you're managing capacity by reducing inputs.

5

Physical symptoms showed up (or got worse). Sleep disruption, digestive issues, jaw tension, chest tightness, and headaches. Your body is trying to speak to you.

6

Performance feels like acting. You show up but it no longer feels real. You play "the one who has it together," and the gap between this role and your true self is exhausting.
