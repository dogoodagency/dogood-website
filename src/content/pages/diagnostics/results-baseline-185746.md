## D. Warning signals to watch
If you start experiencing any of the below consistently, it usually means you’re drifting into the next higher Pressure Index.​ This is when the nervous system starts needing more effort to produce the same stability.

1

You delay one vital decision because of how you feel. This usually means avoidance is now regulating your behavior, not values.

2

Your baseline becomes “jagged” (calm feels harder to access).Early sign that your nervous system is spending more time in mobilization.

3

Rest stops working like it used to (you rest, but don’t feel restored).That’s often the first indicator your recovery inputs aren’t matching outputs.

4

Your private self-talk sharpens: “I should be able to handle this.”Increased internal pressure tends to precede increased external friction.

5

Relationships feel slightly more transactional: efficient, less intimate, less repair. Not a rupture—just less warmth, less curiosity, less slack.

6

You start “micro-dosing” to stay level (caffeine, work, scrolling). This is how founders self-medicate with rising pressure.
