## D. Warning signals to watch
If you start experiencing any of the below in the next 7 days, it usually means you're moving into the highest PSI band — where identity unravels, decisions become impossible, and relationships rupture in ways that are far harder to repair.

1

You can't make basic decisions without spiraling. Not "hard decisions." Basic ones. What to eat. Whether to respond to a message. Whether to get out of bed.

2

Your mind starts running catastrophic scenarios on a loop. "What if I lose everything?" / "What if I can't recover?" / "What if this is permanent?" — and you can't stop the loop.

3

You feel nothing (or everything) with no middle ground. Emotional flatness interrupted by sudden overwhelm. This is nervous system dysregulation. It's not "just stress."

4

Relationships feel transactional, or you've stopped trying. Not because you don't care — because you don't have anything left to give. And they feel it.

5

You imagine disappearing. Not hopeless, earth-leaving thoughts (if so, seek help) — but persistent thoughts of "what if I just walked away from all of it?"

6

Physical symptoms become impossible to ignore. Chest tightness, persistent nausea, and heart palpitations. Your body is forcing the call your mind has been avoiding.
