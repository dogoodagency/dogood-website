# FRP-Fit Call

This call is for founders who’ve already applied for Stage 1: The Reset (30 days).

It's a two-way conversation to determine fit.

What we'll cover:

→ Your situation and what's actually going on

→ How Stage 1 works, what you'll walk away with, and timing

→ Test fit for FRP (privacy, intensity, readiness, time)

→ Answer all of your questions

→ Decision whether this is the right step for both of us

Next step:

(A) proceed toward Stage 1 onboarding, or

(B) a clear 'not a fit / not now' decision

Before the call:

Review your application and be ready with your questions.
