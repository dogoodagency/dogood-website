# Team Readiness & Timing Call

This call is for COOs, CHROs, and L&D leaders who want culture, SOP, and execution work. This call confirms the right sequence before doing anything org-wide.

We only take team engagements after founder-first work is complete, because that’s what makes downstream change sustainable. Book a short call, and we’ll confirm readiness, timing, and next steps.

On this call, we’ll cover:

- What you want to improve (culture, SOPs, upskilling, onboarding)
- What’s blocking follow-through right now
- Whether founder readiness is already in place, and next step

Founder-first reminder:
If you’re the founder, the prerequisite is → [Stage 1: The Intensive (30 days](/how-it-works)) before any team engagement.
