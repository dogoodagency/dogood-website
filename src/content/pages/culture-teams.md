# We work with your team, once the founder is ready. Typically 4–6 months

Culture transformation, staff upskilling & operational excellence for growth-stage companies — but only after the foundation is set.

We help growth-stage companies strengthen culture, execution, and leadership. But we do things differently. We only take team engagements after the founder has done the initial work. Once the foundation is set (typically 4–6 months), we step in with culture build, SOP development, staff upskilling, and talent integration. [Here is why](/culture-teams#anchor-2) →

THE REASON WE SAY: NO

We don't take team engagements unless the founder has worked with us first. No workshops. No culture audits. No org-wide initiatives —

until the foundation is set.

Why?

We've seen it too many times —

leadership programs, engagement initiatives, and culture work that doesn't stick.

Not because the work was wrong, but because the person at the top wasn't ready.

Their stress was still the team's stress. Their confusion was still the company's confusion.

We work with founders first because that's where real change starts. Once the founder is ready — typically 4–6 months — our team steps in to extend that transformation across your organization.
