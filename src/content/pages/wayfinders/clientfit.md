# Client-Fit Process

We match you to a Wayfinder for fit first because outcomes depend on the relationship and working style. Once the fit is satisfied, we prioritize proximity/time zone to keep scheduling clean and travel sustainable.

How it works

1. Apply for Stage 1: You share what’s happening (health, relationship, identity, pressure) and what you need most.

2. After the FRP-Fit Call: We confirm this is the right container: private, intensive, founder-first, and you can protect the required time.

3. Match to your Wayfinder: We match based on your situation and what you respond to: direct vs gentle, highly structured vs more spacious, crisis containment needs, and your stage (still running vs post-exit).

4. Optimize for proximity: After fit, we prioritize geographic/time zone alignment to reduce friction. We also keep travel within wellbeing guardrails (no extreme routes; long-haul is exception-only).

5. Early checkpoint + correction if needed: If within the first two sessions (within 7 days of your first private 1:1 session), the match feels wrong, we attempt to correct it promptly. If it can’t be corrected without disruption, you have the option to exit via the [Clarity‑First Guarantee](/clarity-first-guarantee-terms) (as long as you’re within the guarantee window and eligibility requirements are met).
