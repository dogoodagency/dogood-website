# Seb | Asia-Pacific Hub

![Seb Wichmann](/media/wayfinders-seb-portrait.png)

# (+ United States)

1 Why founders come to me

So you know I understand the moment you’re in — and what this work is actually for.

I believe I’ve been where you are. I built a successful business that nearly broke me — then life came to an unapologetic halt too and forced a reset where I lost everything. I felt lost, beaten up, and genuinely unsure if I could get back up. The work I’m here for started when I did the hardest project: me.

2 What I’m known for

The edge you get with me — beyond “right questions.”

I have an intuitive gift for sensing what’s needed to take you to the next level — whether that’s greater clarity or empowered decisions — so you can thrive and live a fulfilled life. I’m great at what I do, and my clients often become lifelong friends and close allies. Not because I’m trying to be liked — but because truth and authenticity create results that change the relationship too.

3 How I work with you

What it feels like to be in the room with me

I can be both nurturing and blunt when it’s warranted. I sense when people buy into their own limiting story of what they cannot do — and if it’s for your benefit, I’ll call out what no one else will tell you. I’m interested in rapid transformation to create clarity, so you can make empowered decisions fast again.

4 Whom I'm the best fit for

So you know quickly if this is “your kind of work

Over the years, I’ve worked a lot with founders who’ve built USD 5M+ companies while their life around the business isn’t all that much fun — broken relationships, health scares, or an inner calm that’s… questionable. If you have a problem in life that you’ve been rolling around with, I can help. If you’re still running, I help you reclaim clarity without stepping back. If you’re post-exit (or close), I help you rebuild identity so the next chapter feels clear and intentional.

5 Why I can hold real stakes

Credibility, fast — so you don’t have to “take a leap of faith.”

I’ve spent 14+ years helping founders and executive teams create major shifts — in companies and in life. I’ve delivered 150+ learning initiatives in 35+ countries, totaling USD 16.5M in enterprise value. I’ve delivered workshops and worked 1:1 with 100+ founders and senior executives — from startups to iconic brands like Adobe, Vodafone, Siemens, Johnson & Johnson, Rolls‑Royce, and Upwork — on business strategy and identity shifts.

6 What I have been trained in

Enough authority to trust me and the method — without hiding behind credentials.

I’m certified as a Master NLP Practitioner and Master Trainer (advanced facilitation), with organizational development studies via Stanford and Harvard programs. The point isn’t credentials, however. It’s this: I use practical, proven methods that stand up in real life — when you’re tired, stressed, and still responsible for a company and family. I won the “Super-Coach Australia” award in 2014, authored Instant Silence, and founded DoGood Agency in 2019 — focused on culture excellence and operational efficiency, to transform companies globally to be profitable and do good.

7 From where I travel

So scheduling is simple and the partnership is sustainable.

I’m located in Australia. If you reside in New Zealand, Southeast Asia, Mexico, the U.S. or Canada, I will come and see you during our 1:1 intensives. If you’re in Europe, we’ll look at what’s realistic (I have family in Germany) — without forcing something that doesn’t make sense.

8 What I'm here to do & my hobbies

So you meet the real me — not just the professional version.

I’m a human lover. I see myself as a visionary. I love entrepreneurship because it serves the world. I’m a passionate Ironman 70.3 triathlon athlete, obsessed with human potential. And, I believe I’m here to be a secret agent of truth, love, and dedication — in service of lifting awareness for humanity.

9 What I wish I could tell you now

One thing, straight from me to you.

I’m not here to motivate you –– that’s all beneath the surface. I’m here to help you create the incredible change you want, so you can live a life full of zest and vigor. You don’t need more pressure; you need clarity you can trust and a path you can actually follow. I’m here to help you get there. I believe in you.

Seb
