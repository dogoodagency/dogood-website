## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels and scripts from embedded content, work in a similar way. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

DoGood OÜ uses cookies on [www.dogoodagency.co](/). This policy explains which ones, why, and how you can control them. [Our Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains how we handle personal data more generally.

## Our legal basis

Analytics and advertisement cookies load only if you agree. This follows the EU ePrivacy rules as applied in Estonia, and the GDPR. Where these cookies involve personal data, our legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The live-chat widget loads on every visit without waiting for functional-cookie consent, as described below.

Strictly necessary cookies do not need your consent. Without them, the site or a service you asked for would not work.

## Things that set no cookies on our site

- **Fonts.** Our fonts (Montserrat and Mansalva) are served from our own site. They set no cookies and send no data to a font provider.
- **Newsletter sign-up.** Our footer sign-up form sends your details straight to MailerLite. It uses no MailerLite script and sets no cookies on our site.
- **Application forms.** Our application and diagnostic forms are links to Jotform's site (form.jotform.com). When you open one, you are on Jotform's site and its own cookie policy applies.
- **Links to YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Amazon Music and LinkedIn.** These are plain links. They set no cookies until you leave our site. Any YouTube video we embed uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) or loads only when you click it.

## How to change or withdraw your consent

You can change or withdraw consent through the cookie banner. This page explains the cookie groups so you can review your preferences. The live-chat widget loads independently of functional-cookie consent.

If you withdraw your consent, this does not affect anything that happened before you withdrew.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block all cookies, some parts of the site, such as chat, booking or the podcast player, may not work.

Cookies set by tawk.to, and by any site you visit through our links, such as Jotform, YouTube or Spotify, follow that provider's own cookie and privacy policies.

## Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when we change the tools on our site or when the law changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. If a change needs your consent again, we will ask through the cookie banner.
