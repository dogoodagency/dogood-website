## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels and scripts from embedded content, work in a similar way. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

DoGood OÜ uses cookies on [www.dogoodagency.co](https://www.dogoodagency.co). This policy explains which ones, why, and how you can control them. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) (Part 1) explains how we handle personal data more generally.

## Our legal basis

We only use cookies that are not strictly necessary if you agree. This follows the EU ePrivacy rules as applied in Estonia, and the GDPR. Where cookies involve personal data, our legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). [ADVISER: confirm the Estonian provision that implements Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC]

## How to change or withdraw your consent

You can reopen the cookie banner at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the site footer. There you can accept, decline or change your choices for each group.

If you withdraw your consent, this does not affect anything that happened before you withdrew.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block all cookies, some parts of the site, such as chat, booking or forms, may not work.

Cookies set by third parties (such as tawk.to, MailerLite, TidyCal, Jotform, YouTube and Google) follow each provider's own cookie and privacy policies.

## Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when we change the tools on our site or when the law changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date. If a change needs your consent again, we will ask through the cookie banner.
