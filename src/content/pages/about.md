# We exist for life after the company.

DoGood is a private partnership for founders who have exited, or are getting ready to, and do not want life after the company to become another version of the life they left behind.

You apply for Stage 1, The Reset, a private 30-day engagement. If it fits, Stage 2, The Build, follows by invitation only.

## Why we exist

A founder's message, from Seb:

"We exist because the support founders need after exit is rarely found in the usual places.

Executive coaches focus on performance. Therapists help people process what is difficult. Both can have value. But neither is built for the moment when the company is no longer central, and you are working out what genuinely belongs next.

You cannot solve that simply by finding another strategy or filling it with more projects.

I know what it costs when the company becomes too much of who you are, and when the old patterns follow you long after the company is gone.

DoGood is what I wish had existed then: a private partnership for founders who do not want life after exit to become another version of the life they left behind."

Seb & the entire DoGood team

## Why 'DoGood'?

Because we love entrepreneurship.

We love what founders build. We love what a great company can create for a family, a team, a community, and the world.

And the best founders we know are not only trying to build something successful. They want to do good.

Good for themselves. Good for their families. Good for their teams. Good for the world.

That is why we do this work.

DoGood believes that success should not come at the expense of the people and parts of life that matter most.
