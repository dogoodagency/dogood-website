# When the deal closes, the hard part starts.

For exit and succession advisors, wealth advisors and family offices.

You helped your client through the transaction. What comes next is personal: more freedom than they expected, and less certainty about what to do with it.

You stay the trusted advisor. We provide a private place for the part that is not financial or legal: helping them see what genuinely belongs in life after exit.

---

## Why refer

Your client's deal can close and the hard part can still be starting.

The company gave their days structure, urgency, and a place to put their energy. Without it, many founders find the freedom they worked toward does not feel the way they expected. Exit and succession work, and wealth advice, look after the transaction and the money. Very few people help with what happens after.

A trusted introduction means they do not have to look for that help alone, and you remain the person they came from.

---

## What your client gets

## A private 30 days, for the personal side

Stage 1 is a private, confidential 30-day engagement for founders who have exited, or are getting ready to, and do not want to make their next move from the same old patterns.

Over 30 days, we help them see what is really surfacing, separate distraction from genuine desire, and get clear on what needs attention before the next major decision. If it fits, Stage 2 follows by invitation only.

The work is limited to 5 new founders a month. If Stage 1 is not right, we say so, and we point them somewhere better. Stage 1 is covered by our Clarity-First Guarantee.

## How a referral works

## How a referral works

A short, careful path. We never contact your client without you.

### 1. An intro conversation with Seb

A short call to see whether a partnership is a fit, and to walk through how we work together. This is also when we share the details of our Referral Partner Agreement.

### 2. You introduce the founder, with their permission

If your client is open to it, you make the introduction. We never approach a founder without that introduction, and we never ask you to make one without their permission.

### 3. A private Fit Call

We speak with the founder about whether Stage 1 is right for them. You do not need to be on this call. If it is not a fit, we say so clearly, and we tell you.

### 4. We keep you informed, with the founder's consent

If they go ahead, we keep you informed of the broad shape of things. We do not share confidential details from the work itself.

### 5. Stage 1 starts

The founder begins a private 30-day engagement. You stay the trusted advisor.

## FAQ

## Questions advisors ask

### Will you contact my client without me?

No. We never contact a founder without your introduction, and we only ask you to introduce them with their permission.

### Who is a good fit to introduce?

Founders who have exited or are getting ready to, and are no longer in the day-to-day (a board or advisory role is fine). This is often a fit when they have more freedom than before, and it does not feel the way they expected. If you are unsure, we can talk it through on the intro call.

### What should I tell them?

That DoGood is a private 30-day engagement for founders navigating life after exit, and that you would like to introduce them, if they are willing. We can give you a short way to say this on the intro call.

### How does the referral fee work?

There is a referral fee, paid under a written Referral Partner Agreement. The details are in that agreement, and we share them on the intro call. We do not publish amounts on this page.

### Will I know how the work is going?

Yes, with the founder's consent. We keep you informed of the broad shape of things. We do not share confidential details from the sessions.

### Do you give financial or legal advice?

No. We do not give financial or legal advice, and we do not poach. You stay the trusted advisor. We work only on the personal side of life after exit.
