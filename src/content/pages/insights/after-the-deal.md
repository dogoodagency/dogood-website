# After the deal closes: a conversation for advisors on the personal side

The transaction can be done and your client can still be asking what comes next. You do not have to step into work that is not yours to open that conversation.

A simple question is enough: what feels different now that the deal is closed, and what do they not want the next chapter to become.

If they want a private place for that, you can point them to a short conversation with us. You stay their advisor.
