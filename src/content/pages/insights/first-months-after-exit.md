# Why the first months after exit can feel harder than the years that came before

You thought stepping away would feel like arrival. For many founders, what arrives first is the loss of structure, and a quieter question than what to start next.

The company had given the days a shape. Meetings, decisions, and other people needing an answer. When that shape goes, the quiet can feel heavier than the work did.

This is a picture of those first months, not a plan for what you should build next. The useful move is to notice what the quiet is asking, before you fill it.
