# When the company is no longer the centre, who are you now?

After years of being the founder, a quieter calendar can feel like a quieter self. Identity often lags behind the legal exit.

The role carried a name, a team, and a reason to get up. When the role changes, the person underneath is still there, and often less rehearsed.

Before you fill the space, it is worth asking what still feels like yours, and what was only the company's.
