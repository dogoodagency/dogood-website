# Get clear on where you stand before your next move.

The Founder Diagnostic™ is a private, 5-10 minute check for founders who have exited, or are getting ready to. Use it to separate story from reality, name what is happening, and choose a next step with less noise.

It is not medical advice or a diagnosis. Not therapy. Not a psychological service.

*If you're feeling severely overwhelmed or can't cope any longer, we recommend you reach out to a local emergency service for help.*

## How it works

- It takes 5-10 minutes.
- Respond based on the past 7-14 days. Don't recall your best day or your worst day.
- Choose the response that feels most accurate, even if you could justify it otherwise.
- You'll see your results and next steps on the results page immediately after you submit.
- We ask for your name and email so we can send your results and action plan, and route any follow-up. This is private. No one will know you're here.

Founder-focused. Not therapy, not medical advice, not psychological services. No pitch. No judgment.

## What you'll get

This diagnostic is designed to point you to the right next step, based on what's happening now, not the story you tell yourself about it.

- A single score from 0 to 100, looking at energy capacity, decision clarity, health, relationships, and sense of direction.
- A picture of where you stand (your diagnostic stage, 1-5), based on what feels uncomfortably accurate now.
- Your urgency window.
- Clear next steps on your results page, right after you submit.
- An action plan sent to your email, plus ongoing support by email based on your situation, all free of charge.

## Who this is for

This is for founders who've exited or meaningfully stepped away, and for founders getting ready to. It also works for founders or co-founders still running the company, and for founders who have stepped back or are between companies, exploring new projects personally, professionally, or both.

## This is not the right tool if...

- You're a CHRO, CPO, L&D lead, or other people leader looking for internal organizational support to manage talent, boost employee engagement, and drive cultural change. Continue [here](/contact).
- You're not a founder, and you're looking for personal support to navigate career challenges, build leadership skills, or balance work and life in a demanding environment.
- The business is on fire with multiple urgent issues, but you're personally fine, managing to stay calm, focused, and resilient despite the business-only turmoil.
