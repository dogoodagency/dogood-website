## The Business Systems Toolkit for Founders
When scaling breaks operations, top talent leaves, and you’re firefighting instead of leading.

You're in growth pain — or decline pain. Either way, it's operational:

Hiring the wrong people (or can't hire fast enough)

Middle managers who can't manage

No SOPs, so everything depends on you

Communication breakdowns across teams

Top talent quietly disengaging or leaving

Workflows that worked before scaling but now fail

More hustle won't fix this. You need systems: hiring frameworks, training pathways, operational SOPs, and retention strategies that let you scale without breaking.
