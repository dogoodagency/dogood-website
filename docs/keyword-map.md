# Keyword map (20 Nov 2026)

One main search phrase per indexable page, so no two pages compete. Volumes are US monthly
searches from DataForSEO via Manifold (19 Nov 2026); "AI" is monthly AI-engine prompt volume.
"–" means too small to measure. Page wording is not changed for keywords without Seb's OK;
titles and descriptions carry the phrase.

## What the data says

- The founder side of this niche barely registers in classic search: "life after selling a
  business", "what to do after selling your business", "depression after selling business"
  all sit at about 10 searches a month in the US. Founders ask these questions in AI chats,
  to their advisors and on LinkedIn, not in Google.
- The advisor side is where measurable demand is: "CEPA" 6,600 (AI 521), "exit planning
  institute" 1,900, "exit planning" 720, "certified exit planning advisor" 720, "exit planning
  advisor(s)" 210, "business exit planning services" 170. CEPA and EPI terms are
  mostly people looking for EPI itself, so we don't target them (see "Don't target" in the Google Doc).
- Coaching categories with AI demand: "CEO coaching" 1,600 (AI 269), "founder coach" 1,600,
  "retirement coaching" 390 (AI 291), "transition coaching" 260, "founder community" 90 (AI 157),
  "founder retreat" 40 (AI 42).
